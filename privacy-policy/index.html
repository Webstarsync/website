Privacy Policy WebStarSync

Effective Date: September 29, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy governs the use of the WebStarSync application ("the App"). The App is an integration tool designed to fetch reviews from the Google Business API and sync them directly into Webflow CMS. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data when you authorize the App to connect your Google and Webflow accounts.

2. Information We Access and Collect

To provide the synchronization service, WebStarSync requires authorization via OAuth 2.0 for both Google and Webflow. We access and process the following information:

  • Google Business Profile Data: Specifically, we read customer reviews and basic profile information from the Google Business locations you authorize.
  • Webflow CMS Data: We access your Webflow site's CMS collections and schema to create, update, or map the synced reviews.
  • Authentication Tokens: We securely store OAuth 2.0 access and refresh tokens for both Google and Webflow to maintain the automated synchronization process without requiring manual logins.

3. How We Use Your Data

The data we access is used strictly to provide the App's core functionality:

  • Fetching new or updated customer reviews from your Google Business Profile.
  • Transforming and pushing those reviews as CMS items into your connected Webflow project.
  • Logging synchronization status (e.g., success or error logs) to ensure the service runs reliably.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use)

WebStarSync's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically:

  • We only read Google Business reviews to transfer them to your designated Webflow CMS.
  • We do not use your Google data for targeted advertising, nor do we sell, rent, or trade this data to third-party data brokers.
  • Human access to your data is strictly limited to troubleshooting integration errors, and only with your explicit consent.

5. Data Storage and Third-Party Transfer

WebStarSync acts as a bridge. While we store your OAuth authentication tokens securely to keep the connection alive, the actual review data (text, author names, ratings) is transmitted directly to Webflow. By using the App, you acknowledge that your Google reviews will be stored within your Webflow account and become subject to Webflow’s Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

6. Revoking Access and Data Deletion

You maintain full control over your accounts. You can revoke WebStarSync's access at any time:

  • For Google: By visiting your Google Account Security settings (https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
  • For Webflow: By removing the App from your Webflow Workspace Integrations settings.

Upon revoking access, WebStarSync will immediately lose the ability to sync data, and we will delete your authentication tokens from our systems.

7. Contact Information

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at: contact@webstarsync.com.
